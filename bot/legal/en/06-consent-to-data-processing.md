# Consent to the Processing of Personal Data

**Revision:** 10 August 2026
**Operator:** the Various VPN service

*This is a translation provided for convenience. The Russian version is the
binding one; in case of any discrepancy the Russian text prevails.*

---

By starting to use the Various VPN service, the user, in accordance with
Federal Law No. 152-FZ of 27.07.2006 "On Personal Data", freely, of their own
will and in their own interest, gives the Operator consent to process their
personal data on the terms set out below.

## 1. Data covered

Consent is given to the processing of:

- the user's Telegram identifier (numeric ID);
- the interface language;
- subscription details: plan, start and end dates, payment status;
- the access parameters issued by the Operator;
- the contents of support enquiries;
- payment details to the extent passed on by the payment service.

Consent **does not extend** to traffic logs, browsing history, the contents of
transmitted data, DNS queries, IP addresses, or location data: the Operator
does not collect them.

## 2. Purposes of processing

- providing access to the service and keeping it working;
- tracking the subscription term and status;
- accepting and confirming payments;
- answering support enquiries;
- meeting the requirements of the legislation of the Russian Federation.

## 3. Actions and methods of processing

Collection, recording, systematisation, accumulation, storage, updating,
retrieval, use, blocking, deletion and destruction — by automated means and
otherwise.

## 4. Disclosure to third parties

No disclosure takes place, except to payment services to the extent needed to
process a payment, and in cases expressly provided for by the legislation of
the Russian Federation.

No cross-border transfer takes place.

## 5. Term and withdrawal of consent

5.1. Consent is valid for the period the service is used and for 12 months
thereafter.

5.2. Consent may be withdrawn at any time through the Telegram bot
https://t.me/variousvpnbot.

5.3. Withdrawing consent ends the provision of the service: without processing
the data listed in section 1 the service cannot be delivered.

5.4. After withdrawal the data is deleted within no more than 30 days, except
for information the Operator is required by law to retain.

## 6. Confirmation

The user confirms that they:

- have read the Privacy Policy;
- have provided accurate data;
- have the legal capacity needed to give this consent.
