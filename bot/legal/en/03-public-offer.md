# Various VPN Public Offer

**Revision:** 10 August 2026

This document is a public offer under Article 437 of the Civil Code of the
Russian Federation and contains a proposal to conclude a contract for the
provision of services for a fee on the terms set out below.

---

## 1. Definitions

1.1. **Provider** — the party named in the header of this document.

1.2. **Customer** — an individual who has accepted the terms of this offer.

1.3. **Service** — provision of access to Various VPN servers for the paid
term.

1.4. **Acceptance** — payment for the Service. From the moment of acceptance
the contract is deemed concluded on the terms of this offer.

## 2. Subject of the contract

2.1. The Provider gives the Customer access to servers for establishing an
encrypted connection, and the Customer pays for the Service.

2.2. The Service comprises: access parameters (keys and a subscription link),
use of the Provider's servers for the paid term, and technical support through
the Telegram bot.

## 3. How the Service is delivered

3.1. The Service is delivered through the Telegram bot
https://t.me/variousvpnbot.

3.2. Access parameters are issued automatically as soon as payment is
confirmed.

3.3. The Service is deemed properly delivered from the moment the access
parameters are passed to the Customer.

3.4. To use the Service the Customer needs a device with a supported operating
system and a working internet connection. The Provider does not supply these.

## 4. Price and payment

4.1. Current plan prices are stated in the Telegram bot and form an integral
part of this offer.

4.2. Payment is made by the methods available in the bot, as 100 % prepayment.

4.3. The Customer's payment obligation is deemed fulfilled once the funds reach
the Provider.

4.4. The Provider may change prices. A change does not apply to a period
already paid for.

## 5. Refunds

5.1. Refunds are governed by a separate document, the Refund Policy, published
in the Telegram bot.

5.2. The Service is by nature the provision of access. Any use of the issued
access parameters confirms that delivery of the Service has begun.

## 6. Rights and obligations of the parties

6.1. The Provider undertakes to grant access for the paid term and to keep the
servers operational.

6.2. The Customer undertakes to use the Service lawfully and not to pass access
to third parties.

6.3. The Provider may suspend the Service without a refund if the Customer
breaches clause 6.2 or the Terms of Service.

## 7. Liability

7.1. The Service is provided on an "as is" basis.

7.2. The Provider is not responsible for the operation of the Customer's
device, the quality of their internet connection, the availability of
third-party resources, or the Customer's own actions.

7.3. The Provider's aggregate liability is limited to the amount actually paid
by the Customer for the current period.

7.4. The parties are released from liability in circumstances of force majeure,
including acts of state authorities and telecom operators that make delivery of
the Service impossible.

## 8. Personal data

8.1. Personal data is processed in accordance with the Privacy Policy.

8.2. The Provider does not monitor or store the contents of the Customer's
traffic.

## 9. Dispute resolution

9.1. A pre-court claim procedure is mandatory. A claim is submitted through the
Telegram bot and is reviewed within 10 (ten) business days.

9.2. If no agreement is reached, the dispute is referred to a court in
accordance with the legislation of the Russian Federation.

## 10. Term and amendments

10.1. The offer is valid indefinitely until withdrawn by the Provider.

10.2. The Provider may amend the terms unilaterally. A new revision is
published in the Telegram bot and takes effect upon publication.

10.3. Continued use of the Service after a new revision is published means
acceptance of it.
