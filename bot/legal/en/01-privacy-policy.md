## 1. General provisions

1.1. This Policy sets out how information about users of the Various VPN
service (the "Service") — the Telegram bot and the mobile app — is processed
and protected.

1.2. The Policy has been prepared in accordance with the Constitution of the
Russian Federation, Federal Law No. 152-FZ of 27.07.2006 "On Personal Data",
and the Civil Code of the Russian Federation.

1.3. By starting to use the Service, the user confirms that they have read this
Policy and agree with the processing it describes. Anyone who finds any
provision unacceptable should not use the Service.

1.4. The current revision of the Policy is always available in the Service's
Telegram bot.

## 2. Definitions

2.1. **Personal data** — any information relating to a directly or indirectly
identified individual.

2.2. **Processing** — any operation on personal data: collection, recording,
storage, updating, use, deletion.

2.3. **Operator** — the party named in the header of this document, who
organises and carries out the processing.

2.4. **User** — an individual using the Service.

2.5. **Service** — the Various VPN Telegram bot and mobile app, which provide
access to servers for an encrypted connection.

## 3. What information is processed

3.1. The Operator follows the principle of minimisation and processes only what
the Service cannot work without:

- the user's Telegram identifier (numeric ID);
- the chosen interface language;
- subscription details: plan, start and end dates, payment status;
- the technical access parameters (keys and subscription links) issued by the
  Operator;
- support enquiries and the correspondence about them;
- payment details needed to confirm payment, to the extent passed on by the
  payment service.

3.2. The Operator **does not collect or store**:

- traffic logs or the history of visited sites and apps;
- the contents of transmitted data;
- DNS queries;
- users' IP addresses;
- location data;
- contacts, files, messages or any other data from the user's device.

3.3. The mobile app stores settings, the server list and traffic volume
statistics **locally on the user's device**. This information is not sent to
the Operator and is deleted together with the app.

3.4. The Operator does not process special or biometric categories of personal
data, nor data of minors below the age at which they may enter into a contract
on their own.

## 4. Why the information is processed

4.1. The purposes of processing are exhaustive:

- providing access to the Service and keeping it working;
- tracking the subscription term and status;
- accepting and confirming payments;
- answering support enquiries;
- meeting the requirements of the legislation of the Russian Federation.

4.2. The Operator **does not use** user information for advertising, profiling
or behavioural assessment, and does not sell or pass it to third parties for
marketing purposes.

## 5. Grounds for processing

5.1. Processing is carried out on the basis of:

- the user's consent, given when they start using the Service;
- the need to perform a contract to which the user is a party (the Service's
  public offer);
- the requirements of the legislation of the Russian Federation.

## 6. How the information is processed and how long it is kept

6.1. Processing is carried out by automated means and otherwise.

6.2. Information is stored on servers rented by the Operator and is protected
by technical and organisational measures: restricted access, encryption of
management channels, and separation of privileges.

6.3. The retention period is the subscription term plus the following 12
months, needed to confirm settlements and handle any claims. After that period
the information is deleted.

6.4. Information is not passed to third parties, except:

- to payment services, to the extent needed to process a payment;
- in cases expressly provided for by the legislation of the Russian Federation,
  upon a reasoned request from an authorised body.

6.5. No cross-border transfer of personal data takes place. The servers a
user's connection passes through do not record traffic content.

## 7. The user's rights

7.1. The user has the right to:

- find out what data about them is being processed;
- require inaccurate data to be corrected;
- withdraw consent to processing;
- require their data to be deleted;
- appeal against the Operator's actions to an authorised body or in court.

7.2. Enquiries are submitted through the Service's Telegram bot. A response is
provided within the period set by law.

7.3. Withdrawing consent ends the provision of the Service: without processing
the information listed in section 3 the service cannot be delivered.

## 8. The Operator's obligations

8.1. The Operator undertakes to:

- process information only for the purposes named in section 4;
- take measures to protect information against unlawful access;
- not disclose information to third parties, except in the cases in clause 6.4;
- notify the authorised body of incidents in the manner and within the periods
  set by law.

## 9. Cookies and analytics

9.1. The Service does not use cookies, advertising identifiers, or external
systems for analysing user behaviour.

## 10. Changes to the Policy

10.1. The Operator may amend the Policy. A new revision is published in the
Service's Telegram bot and takes effect upon publication.

10.2. Continued use of the Service after publication means acceptance of the
