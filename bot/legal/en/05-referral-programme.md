# Various VPN Referral Programme Terms

**Revision:** 10 August 2026
**Organiser:** the Various VPN service

*This is a translation provided for convenience. The Russian version is the

## 1. General provisions

1.1. The referral programme rewards users who recommend the Service to others.

1.2. Participation is voluntary and free. Taking part means accepting these
Terms.

## 2. Definitions

2.1. **Participant** — a user of the Service who has been issued a personal
referral link.

2.2. **Invitee** — a new user who starts using the Service for the first time
through a Participant's referral link.

2.3. **Reward** — additional subscription days credited to the Participant.

## 3. How it works

3.1. The Participant receives a personal referral link in the Telegram bot.

3.2. The Invitee must follow the link and take out a subscription for the first
time.

3.3. Once the Invitee's payment is confirmed, the Participant is credited with
the reward in the amount stated in the Telegram bot at the time of crediting.

3.4. The reward is credited in subscription days. It cannot be paid out in
money, exchanged, or transferred to another person.

## 4. Restrictions

4.1. No reward is credited if:

- the Invitee has used the Service before;
- the Invitee and the Participant are the same person or use the same device;
- there are signs of artificial inflation: bulk registration, automated
  actions, use of someone else's data;
- the Invitee's payment was refunded or cancelled.

4.2. Where abuse is found, the Organiser may cancel credited rewards and
exclude the Participant from the programme.

4.3. The referral link may not be distributed in ways that break the law or the
rules of any platform, including spam.

## 5. The Organiser's rights

5.1. The Organiser may change the reward amount and the crediting conditions,
and may end the programme at any time.

5.2. A new revision is published in the Telegram bot and takes effect upon
publication. Rewards already credited are retained.

## 6. Personal data

6.1. Running the programme involves processing the user's Telegram identifier
and the inviter–invitee link. Processing is described in the Privacy Policy.

## 7. Final provisions

7.1. This programme is not a lottery, a game of chance, a public competition,
or a promotional event within the meaning of advertising legislation.

7.2. Disputes are handled through the Telegram bot within 10 (ten) business
days.
