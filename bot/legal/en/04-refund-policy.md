# Various VPN Refund Policy

**Revision:** 10 August 2026
**Provider:** the Various VPN service

*This is a translation provided for convenience. The Russian version is the
binding one; in case of any discrepancy the Russian text prevails.*

---

## 1. General provisions

1.1. This Policy sets out how refunds for a Various VPN subscription are made
and forms an integral part of the public offer.

1.2. The Policy takes into account Law of the Russian Federation No. 2300-1 of
07.02.1992 "On Protection of Consumer Rights".

## 2. Right to cancel

2.1. The Customer may cancel the Service at any time.

2.2. On cancellation the Provider refunds the value of the **unused** part of
the paid period, less costs actually incurred.

2.3. The refund amount is calculated pro rata to the number of whole unused
days of the paid period.

## 3. When a full refund is made

3.1. A full refund is made if:

- the Service was not delivered through the Provider's fault (access was never
  granted);
- the Service was unavailable through the Provider's fault for more than 72
  consecutive hours within the paid period, and the Customer contacted support
  during that period;
- payment was made twice in error for the same period.

## 4. When no refund is made

4.1. No refund is made if:

- access was suspended because the Customer breached the Terms of Service;
- the unavailability was caused by the Customer's equipment, their internet
  provider, the actions of third parties, or force majeure;
- the paid period has fully expired;
- the Service was received under a promotion, bonus or the referral programme
  and was not paid for with money.

4.2. The Customer being unable to open a particular third-party resource is not
a defect of the Service: the Provider does not control third-party resources.

## 5. How to apply

5.1. A refund request is submitted through the Telegram bot
https://t.me/variousvpnbot, stating the payment date, the amount and the
reason.

5.2. The review period is 10 (ten) business days from receipt of the request.

5.3. The refund is issued by the same method used for payment, within 10 (ten)
business days of the decision. The time it takes to reach the Customer's
account depends on their bank or payment service.

5.4. The Provider may request the information needed to identify the payment.

## 6. Final provisions

6.1. The Provider may amend this Policy. A new revision is published in the
Telegram bot and takes effect upon publication.

6.2. The laws of the Russian Federation apply to the relations of the parties.
